I have a small surprise for you...

For You...

If someone had told me three years ago that a random person from a Telegram study group would become one of the most important people in my life... I probably wouldn't have believed them.

It all started so randomly. A study group... a few conversations... then Snapchat... And somehow, those small conversations slowly became a part of everyday life.
Today... it's been almost three years. It's funny how we're still a long-distance friendship. We've never shared the same classroom, never met every day like most friends do... Yet somehow, our friendship survived every distance.

There are days when we don't even realize how much time we've spent talking. Sometimes one call becomes hours. Sometimes one random message makes the whole day better. I honestly don't know how it happened... But somewhere along the way, you became one of those people I genuinely don't want to lose.
I know there's something I should also say... Sometimes I joke too much. Sometimes I say things without thinking. And sometimes... I end up hurting you without ever wanting to.

If I've ever made you feel bad, I'm really sorry. It was never my intention. You deserve smiles, not reasons to overthink because of me.
Thank you... For staying. For listening. For laughing at my stupid jokes. For tolerating my nonsense. For making ordinary days feel less ordinary.

I don't know what life has planned for us. Maybe things will change. Maybe they'll stay exactly the same. But one thing I hope never changes... is this friendship. Because honestly... it's one of the best things that ever happened to me.

Thank you for these beautiful three years. And thank you... for being you. ❤️
